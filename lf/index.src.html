Features

Everything your phone and PC can do together.

Each feature below plays out on a 3D model of the two devices. The animations illustrate how a feature works. Features that are still being built are clearly marked.

Fast device pairing

Connect in Seconds.

Pair your Android phone and Windows PC quickly and securely with a 4-digit PIN.

  • Quick PIN pairing: your PC shows a 4-digit PIN, you enter it on your phone.
  • Secure device pairing between your own devices.
  • Automatic reconnection after you've paired once.
Available
Clipboard synchronization

Copy Here. Paste Anywhere.

Synchronize clipboard content between your Android phone and Windows PC.

  • Bidirectional: copy on Windows, paste on Android, and the other way around.
  • Text and clipboard previews on both devices.
  • Travels over your existing LinkFlow connection.
Available
File sharing

Your Files. Everywhere.

Transfer files between your phone and PC through your connected devices.

  • PC to phone and phone to PC.
  • A live progress indicator for every transfer.
  • Clear confirmation when a transfer completes.
Available
Phone camera sharing

Your Phone Camera. On Your PC.

Use your Android phone camera as a camera source on your Windows computer.

  • Share the phone camera with your PC.
  • See a live camera preview on the computer.
  • A simple phone-to-PC camera workflow.
Available
Screen sharing

Your Screens. Connected.

Phone to PC: fast QR code mirror

Scan the code on your PC with your phone and the phone screen appears inside a window on Windows. This mode uses wireless debugging (ADB over Wi-Fi) where required.

PC to Phone

Stream your Windows desktop to your phone and see the PC screen right inside the phone.

Phone as a second screen

Extended display: your phone acts as an additional display for Windows, so you can drag a window from your main monitor onto the phone.

Available

Phone as trackpad

Control Your PC From Your Phone.

Turn your Android smartphone into a wireless trackpad for Windows.

  • Move the cursor with your finger.
  • Tap to click.
  • Scroll with touch gestures.
Available
Controller

Your Phone. Your Controller.

Use your Android phone as a virtual controller for your Windows PC, with haptic feedback and gyro.

  • Haptic feedback as you press.
  • Gyro: tilt your phone to steer or aim.
  • Eight layouts, including your own.

Xbox layout

Twin sticks, D-pad, A/B/X/Y buttons, bumpers and triggers.

PS5 layout

PlayStation-style face buttons, sticks, D-pad and shoulder buttons.

Racing controller

Tilt your phone to steer, with gas and brake pedals and shift paddles.

FPS controller

A movement stick, a look area, and fire, aim, jump, reload and crouch buttons.

Fight controller

A D-pad with six attack buttons plus special and guard.

Keyboard and mouse

WASD and common keys with a mouse pad and click buttons.

Media controller

Play, pause, skip, volume and seek for whatever is playing on your PC.

Customise

Build your own layout: add buttons, move them around and save the result.

Available
Autotype

Write It Once. Autotype It Anywhere.

Write or paste your text in LinkFlow and let Autotype type it into any window on your Windows PC.

  • A dedicated space to write your text.
  • Types automatically wherever you choose.
  • Start, pause and stop from your phone.
Available
Personalize LinkFlow

Make LinkFlow Yours.

LinkFlow has two themes. Pick a colour on the wheel and watch the apps change instantly. Try it below.

Custom colours apply to Light Mode. Dark Mode maintains its original high-contrast appearance. Save up to three presets and switch between them any time.

Available
